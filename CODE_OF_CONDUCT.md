# VFPC Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project is run in a direct, technical style.
People are expected to be clear, respectful, and focused on solving the problem in front of them.

This is not a place for ego contests, personal drama, or low-effort hostility.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, but do not be abusive.
- Critique code, decisions, and evidence, not people.
- Bring specifics when you disagree.
- Accept correction when you are wrong.
- Keep discussions on-topic and useful.
- Assume good faith until there is a concrete reason not to.
- Leave the repo, issues, and discussions easier to work with than you found them.

## Not Acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, dogpiling, or harassment.
- Trolling, baiting, or picking fights for entertainment.
- Sexualized behavior or unwanted personal attention.
- Bigoted or degrading remarks about any group of people.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion into personal or political arguments.
- Spamming, low-effort noise, or deliberately wasting maintainer time.
- Misrepresenting facts, logs, test results, or prior discussion.

## Maintainer Standard

Maintainers are expected to follow the same rules.
They may be blunt, but they should still be fair, factual, and proportionate.

## Scope

This applies to:

- this repository
- pull requests, issues, discussions, and reviews
- project Discord spaces
- any public interaction clearly made on behalf of the project

## Enforcement

If behavior crosses the line, maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject content, and may limit or end participation.

Possible responses include:

- a private warning
- a public warning
- temporary removal from project spaces
- permanent removal from project spaces

Severity matters. Repeated behavior matters. Whether someone corrects course matters.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening a ticket on the project Discord server:
https://discord.gg/zcgkBfayDz

Reports should include enough detail to investigate properly.
Deliberately false or malicious reports are themselves a conduct problem.

## Final Rule

Treat people like adults.
Do solid technical work.
Do not make the project harder to run.
