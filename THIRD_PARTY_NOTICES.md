## Dependency Management

The direct third-party C++ dependencies used by vFPC are declared in
`vcpkg.json` and are restored using vcpkg.

Additional transitive dependencies, such as zlib, may be installed by
vcpkg as dependencies of those packages.

The vcpkg manifest specifies a baseline so that dependency resolution
can be reproduced for a particular version of the vFPC source.

For build instructions, see `BUILD_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
